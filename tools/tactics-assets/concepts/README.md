# Scenario concept series

Issue #80's Ashen Ford people work is tracked in [townsfolk/README.md](townsfolk/README.md):
12 named NPC briefs, seven paired townsfolk briefs, and an approved Bree anchor
(12 Scenario CU spent). The owner approved 234 CU for the remaining 18 sheets;
Mother Skarrow completed for 13 CU before a provider cooldown stopped the batch.
Total spend is 25 CU; 17 sheets remain with 221 CU authorized.

`scenario-briefs.json` is the prepared, lore-linked request pack for **Scenario**.
Generation receipts belong to their individual batches. See the
[Ashmaw bounty batch](ashmaw/README.md) for its three generated concepts,
approved character designs, full-resolution files and credit accounting.

The first series is 12 images:

- Six environmental concepts: Emberwick/Last Coal, Ashen Ford, Mirewood,
  Sunscar, Frostpeak and Ember Keep.
- Six key character sheets: Halden Varrow, Bree Tallow, Old Maud,
  Mother Skarrow, Maren Sorrel and the Hooded Stranger.

The pack additionally prepares sheets for the other 13 named NPCs, giving 25
original briefs in total. Three additional issue #74 briefs cover Ashmaw, Grisk
and Cinder Hollow. These are later briefs, not an instruction to submit all jobs
at once. All share an original, grounded tactical-fantasy design direction.

Character sheets request matching front, three-quarter and back views, a head
study and equipment/material details. They preserve anatomy, outfit, handedness,
rear-facing garment details and equipment contact. The Stranger stays masked in
every view. The existing generic NPC presets are references, not authority over
the lore: Old Maud and Mother Aubrine must not inherit a male appearance from
their legacy placeholder kits, and Ysolde's placeholder does not establish drow
ancestry. Unspecified physical details are design proposals.

The environmental concepts emphasize readable architecture, material planes,
human scale and usable routes. Warm autumn farmland, old cold ash, a steaming
river and a poor repaired Skarrow camp are specific to the Ashen Ford. The
other regions retain their own distinct climate and magical character.

For generation, inspect the actual Scenario models first. Make one environment
anchor and one representative character sheet, review their design consistency,
then reuse the accepted model/reference settings. Store prompts, model, seeds,
references, returned job/image IDs and outputs together. Show the results as a
contact sheet with full-resolution individual files.

These are painted concept references for later pixel-art work, not drop-in game
textures, sprites, collision data or animation sheets. The prepared pack explicitly
requires Scenario; do not silently substitute another provider.
