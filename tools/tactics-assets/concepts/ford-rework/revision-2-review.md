# Bridge state revision 2

Owner feedback on revision 1: “The differences are not really noticeable, to be honest.”
Revision 1 is retained for provenance; it is not approved for production.

The revision uses one comparison sheet with matching camera, scale and framing.
Remove the scenic landscape and construction insets so the state differences
occupy useful review space. The bridge, piers, deck and gatehouse remain the
same architecture. All outcome states must retain a continuous usable deck.

| State | Large identifying forms |
| --- | --- |
| Fortified | Closed spiked checkpoint; tall ragged hide screens; dark side awning |
| After force | Screens torn down; large smashed barricade heap off the lane; collapsed awning; localized scorch damage |
| After parley and hotheads | Gates folded open; low rolled hides; orderly stake bundles; pale side shelter with modest grain sacks |

Review the result both full-size and with each panel approximately 200 pixels
wide. These differences must be apparent without reading the labels. Check that
the force wreckage and parley gate leaves stay off the lane, and that no outcome
changes the number of arches or destroys the load-bearing structure.

The scorch and shelter dressing are visual proposals, not new lore or committed
environmental mechanics. The force state must retain any separate corpse/story
staging needed by the existing scene. Parley reopening follows the hothead fight.
The original concept approval gate still applies before pixel production.
