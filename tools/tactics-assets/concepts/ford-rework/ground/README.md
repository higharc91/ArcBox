This proposes the ground's material direction: cool charcoal ash with sharp broken forms, subdued autumn grass,
a continuous worn earth road, stone-supported ramp transitions, and a readable wet shore. The bridge is context;
approval here concerns the ash, grass, roads and shores, not a replacement bridge design.

The source of the old softness is recorded in the [Ford report](../../../../../docs/superpowers/reports/2026-11-05-ford-rework/README.md):
tonal source textures and continuous feathering, rather than a missing nearest-filter setting. This is a painted
approval reference. Actual discrete pixels, restrained palette clusters and game-scale transitions come in the native terrain pass.

[#82](https://github.com/higharc91/ArcBox/issues/82) requires new ground art to go through the same concept → pixel approval.
