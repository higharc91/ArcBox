# Storm, Magma and Swamp elemental concepts

First additional-library batch, after the completed 29-design combat pass. Exact briefs, providers, generation IDs and Scenario receipt are in generation-record.json. Scenario generated Storm successfully. Its new-job endpoint then returned HTTP 429 with about 15 minutes of cooldown, so OpenAI generated Magma and Swamp concepts from the approved primary concepts. No PixelLab used.

Storm: slate/indigo continuous wind ribbons, charcoal core, steel/silver crest highlights and narrow white eyes. One joined vortex base, two wind arms, no legs/clothes. Match Gale's proportions; Whirlwind spreads connected coils, Gale Blast thrusts both hands.

Magma: rough charcoal/burgundy basalt boulders with restrained orange/amber fissures and eyes. Two continuously connected heavy arms and feet. Match Stone's hunched anatomy, heavy gait, forward fist, raised-fist Tremor and crossed-arm Aegis. No floating lava rocks, armour or new fire powers.

Swamp: murky olive/peat liquid core, moss-green wave volumes, ochre/cream foam rims and amber eyes. Match Sea's continuous water column/base and joined arms. Wave Slam thrusts a palm; Tidal Crush heaves both arms; Undertow pulls inward. No independent reeds/plants, legs or poison powers.

All use primary creature scale beside the 1.75 m fighter: Storm about 2.3 m, Magma 2.8 m, Swamp 2.4 m. Full backs remain faceless. Concepts guide material and identity; approved runtime poses guide the pixel anatomy. No stat blocks, encounters, rules, lore names or abilities are added.
