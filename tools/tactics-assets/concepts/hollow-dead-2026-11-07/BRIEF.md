# Risen of Frostpeak and wraith

Baseline #28 (`characters.zombie`) and #12 (`bosses.wraith.wraith`). Bestiary sources: `content/tactics/base/lore/codex/bestiary.json`; combat equipment/abilities: `rules/monsters/undead.json` and `rules/abilities/monsters.json`.

The Risen are former travellers of Frostpeak, raised by cold ember, empty rather than cruel. This shared appearance represents one human former Warden pilgrim, not all dwarven and goliath variants mentioned by the bestiary. Intact ashen face, sparse grey hair, moss-olive tunic, patched umber jerkin, faded cream scarf, wrist wraps and muddy boots. Hunched ordinary adult human scale. Empty hands deliver the existing slam attack. Slow heavy shuffle; no gore or glowing eyes.

Wraiths remain after ember-fire consumes every memory: cold, hungry and drawn to the living. Human-sized charcoal-indigo hooded spirit, slate-blue cloth highlights, empty dark hood with restrained grey eye slits, cold grey hands joined to sleeves and a single connected ragged floating tail. No legs, weapons, crown or detached wisps. Physical spectral touch uses one reaching arm; life drain (`bosses.special`) has a separate two-handed gathering/pulling gesture. Existing renderer owns FX. Eight real facings, rear hood seams without faces, no mirroring.
