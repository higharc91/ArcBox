# Basalt, Crystal and Temple Golem concepts

Second additional-library batch, following the completed 29-design combat pass. [Exact briefs](briefs.json) and [provider receipts](receipts.json) preserve prompts and generation IDs. Scenario produced Basalt (8 CU); its next submission returned HTTP 429 with 906 seconds remaining. OpenAI generated Crystal and Temple without retrying during that cooldown. PixelLab was not used.

- **Basalt:** dense charcoal stone, cool slate edge planes, dark recesses, restrained amber eyes and a round front chest core. Distinct from the Magma Earth Elemental: no lava body or external flames.
- **Crystal:** lavender stone chassis with physically joined violet/ice-blue mineral points at crown and shoulders; round violet chest core. No floating shards or disintegrating glass anatomy.
- **Temple:** worn sandstone with ochre core, small moss patches and simple carved forearm bands. No new named civilization, holy powers, vines or scenery.

All retain the approved Granite Golem's party scale and squat two-arm/two-leg proportions. Front cores and eyes turn away in back views. Heavy alternating walking, connected right-fist punches, two-fist Seismic Slam and crossed-forearm Stone Aegis use the existing abilities. Concepts establish material identity; complete approved runtime poses guide the eight-direction pixel sheets. No stat blocks or encounters are added.
