# Black Hound, Grey Hound and Violet Imp concepts

[Shared concept sheet](three-variants.png) · [Exact brief](brief.txt) · [Scenario receipt](receipts.json)

Scenario generated one three-column production sheet with front, side, back, movement and natural-attack studies (Nano Banana 2.1, 2K, 12 CU). No PixelLab used. These are existing library appearances, not new creatures or stat blocks.

Black Hound uses a charcoal coat, umber muzzle/lower legs and restrained grey highlights. Grey Hound uses slate/silver with a dark saddle. Both match the approved Tan Hunting Hound: lean scent-hound proportions, floppy ears, four attached legs, rooted low curved tail and a weathered leather collar with a dull front/side buckle. Rear collars remain plain leather. Art scale guidance: 0.65 m at shoulder, roughly 1.05 m nose-to-rump beside a 1.75 m fighter.

Violet Imp matches the approved Crimson Bound Imp at about 0.8 m to the horns. Lavender skin/plum shadows, ivory horns, amber eyes, plum membrane wings, waistwrap/rope and a rooted spade tail. Both wings stay attached and present with natural occlusion; full backs do not copy the front face or waist detail. Restrained wing-assisted stepping keeps feet close to the ground. The right hand gathers and releases the existing natural hellfire bolt; no new ability or projectile is painted into body frames.

Whole approved primary poses guide the pixel anatomy and action geometry. Hounds bite close to the ground; the imp casts from an attached hand. No added armor, weapons, named lore, abilities or encounter placements. Down poses retain connected anatomy without gore.
