# Bone Wardens: warrior and archer

Baseline #26 and #27: `characters.skeleton` and `characters.skeleton_archer`. The bestiary calls them the oldest dead of the Wardens' crypts, still in the remnants of their uniforms and held at their posts by the crypt wards. They are empty guards, not gleeful or demonic caricatures.

Shared adult human scale, weathered ivory skull/bones, dark empty sockets, connected jaw/neck/joints, slate-blue split uniform tabard, charcoal light leather, dull brass fittings, brown wraps and boots. The worn badge is unreadable rather than an invented canonical sigil. Rear views show uniform seams and plain belt rather than front buckles. No horns, glowing pupils, gore or random trophies.

Warrior: right-hand straight shortsword, empty left hand, no shield. Archer: left-hand shortbow, right draw hand and a back-mounted quiver. Flying arrows remain owned by the game's projectile renderer. Quiet watching/settling idle rather than biological breathing; four walking/attack keys per direction, one hurt pose and one collapsed pose, all in eight real facings. Down poses keep the articulated figure and weapon together rather than scatter loose bones.
