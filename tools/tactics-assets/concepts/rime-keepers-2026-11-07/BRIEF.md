# Ivo Rime / necromancer and Oswin Hale / lich

Baseline #24 (`characters.necromancer`) and #23 (`characters.lich`). Canon comes from `content/tactics/base/lore/characters.json`; existing equipment/abilities are in `rules/monsters/undead.json`, `rules/monsters/villains.json` and `rules/abilities/monsters.json`.

Ivo is a living Warden-trained physician studying the cold ember after Nell's death. He is exhausted and precise, not a cackling corpse wizard. Use a narrow middle-aged human face, short brown hair with grey temples, dark circles, charcoal-blue travel coat, grey-green light jerkin, faded cream scarf, brown boots and a sealed medicine pouch. The shared generic necromancer appearance and Ivo's named stat block retain their existing shared key.

Oswin is the century-undead Last Warden-Keeper, very tired and burdened by memory. He refused ember-fire. Use a connected ivory human skull with empty sockets, lowered neck cowl, slate-navy Warden robes, worn ivory stole, restrained brass fittings and boots. No crown, horns, fiery eyes or ember staff. The lich preset also serves Oswin's dialogue; no new boss encounter is invented by this art pass.
