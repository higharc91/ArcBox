# Bound Imp concept

Existing generic summoned fiend companion `imp`, baseline #7, sprite `bosses.imp.crimson`. Natural ranged hellfire_bolt weapon, fire damage, no active abilities. No new name, lore, ability, equipment mechanics, balance or encounter placement. Violet library art remains separate.

A small lean crimson fiend with two short swept-back ivory horns, two attached pointed ears, narrow amber eyes with dark pupils/lids, two clawed arms, two digitigrade legs and one low curled tail ending in a small spade. Two burgundy membrane bat wings attach to the upper back, with continuous membranes and finger struts. The concept's wingless rear anatomy study is not a production pose; both wings must remain present in the pixel figures.

A plain ochre/charcoal waistwrap and dark rope, no front buckle copied to the back. Restrained shoulder-driven wing-assisted stepping, not high flight. Right hand gathers at the chest and extends toward the target on release, with elbow/wrist/fingers physically joined. Full-back casting extends away using the same right arm. No free projectile painted into the body sheet; runtime uses existing fire visuals. Hurt folds the wings; down rests/slumps on the ground with all anatomy connected.

Art target about 0.8m to horns beside a 1.75m adult; this is scale guidance, not new canonical lore. Deliberate fine pixel-friendly outline/material hierarchy, warm upper-left key and cool wine shade; no huge eyes or giant demon proportions.
