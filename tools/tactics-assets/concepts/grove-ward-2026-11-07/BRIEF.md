# Grove Sapling

Existing generic summoned plant companion `sapling`, baseline #9, sprite `bosses.sapling.spring`. Its natural vine_lash is bludgeoning, non-ranged and reaches two tiles. No active abilities or named lore are added. Autumn/blossom library variants remain separate.

Short knotted oak/umber trunk and bark face, restrained olive moss, small recessed amber eyes, root feet and branch arms. Two forked crown twigs carry compact spring leaf masses; leaves remain joined to twigs. The successful concept draws the coiled vine on the anatomical LEFT arm (front image-right, rear image-left), despite the draft requesting right. Production follows this consistent concept orientation without mirroring. The vine spirals along that branch arm and continues through the wrist into a physical lash; no detached projectile, staff, carried weapon or magic beam. Plain wood/moss on the back with no copied face.

Art target approximately 0.95m crown-to-floor beside a 1.75m adult; guidance, not new canonical lore. Slow root-footed steps, stable trunk/crown, restrained leaf movement at twig roots. Hurt bends/braces the trunk; down rests the joined plant on its side. No clothing, belt, armor, flowers, fruit or loose leaves.
