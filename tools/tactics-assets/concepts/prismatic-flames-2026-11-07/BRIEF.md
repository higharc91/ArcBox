# Azure, Radiant and Shadow Fire Elemental concepts

[Shared three-variant concept sheet](three-variants.png) · [Exact prompt](brief.txt) · [Scenario receipt](receipts.json)

Scenario generated one production sheet with three separate material columns (Nano Banana 2.1, 2K, 12 CU). Each column develops the same living-flame anatomy in front, side and back views, with common scale beside a human fighter. This is the third additional-library batch after the completed combat-roster pass. PixelLab was not used.

- **Azure:** cobalt/royal-blue inner flames, azure planes and pale cyan edges/eyes.
- **Radiant:** old-gold/amber inner flames, golden-yellow planes and cream edges/eyes; readable detail rather than an overexposed white glow.
- **Shadow:** charcoal/plum core, violet planes and restrained lavender edges/eyes; readable against dark terrain.

All retain the approved Ember Fire Elemental's two continuously joined arms, tapered flame column and curled base. They glide without legs. Full backs have no eyes or front face. Natural strikes push a connected palm forward; Flame Surge gathers then spreads both arms, while Flame Breath braces and leans forward toward its target. No clothes, weapons, independent particles, invented factions or new powers. Pixel action geometry comes from complete approved Ember poses; the concept sheet supplies variant materials.
