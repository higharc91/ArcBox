# Kindled cultist and Maren Sorrel / Ember Hierophant

Baseline #16 and #15, preserving their existing shared combat keys and aliases. Faction lore describes hungry and desperate people, many kind, led by a compassionate and sincere preacher willing to sacrifice lives to relight the Hearthflame. Match that humanity rather than generic demon costumes.

The cultist has an open charcoal hood, rust shoulder stole, split umber travel robe, practical boots and right-hand ritual dagger. Maren is a middle-aged human woman with grey temples and a close low bun, leather chest/forearm armor, maroon mantle, split rust robe and a small caged-ember ashwood staff. The generated concept's **left-hand staff** is adopted over the original draft's right-hand specification; her right hand remains free for spellcasting. Keep each role's handedness fixed in every facing and action.

Eight authored facings, separate idle/walk/physical attack/spellcast/hurt/down, six dialogue expression roles and actual atlas HUD crops. Projectile and spell FX belong to the existing game renderer, not baked floating overlays. No combat/lore/spawn changes; the existing shared cult-leader appearance continues to serve both generic hierophants and Maren's named stat block.
