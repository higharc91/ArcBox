# Air, Earth and Fire Elementals

Three-creature batch following Grove Sapling. Grounded Wardenflame tactical fantasy, crisp shapes and pixel-friendly material ramps. Scenario concept receipts and exact briefs are in generation-record.json. Pixel production follows the concepts, using the refreshed bandit as density reference.

Air: pale blue-grey continuous wind ribbons over a slate core, swept-back crest, front-only narrow eyes, connected arms and one tapered vortex base. No legs. Whirlwind spreads its coils; Gale Blast thrusts both palms forward. The concept's forked lower body in one ability sketch is excluded from production.

Earth: warm ochre sandstone, rough cliff strata, dark umber seams and fixed olive moss; hunched shoulders, connected fists and two heavy planted feet. Distinct from the grey granite golem. Earth Tremor raises then grounds both fists; Stone Aegis crosses the forearms protectively.

Fire: yellow/amber core, orange planes, crimson flame edge, swept-back crest, connected flame arms and a braided flame-column base. No legs or molten armour. Flame Surge opens the arms and flares the body; Flame Breath leans its head and opens its mouth toward the target. Existing runtime effects supply the actual breath cone.

Neutral scale beside a 1.75m fighter: Air roughly2.3m, Earth2.8m, Fire2.4–2.5m. Front-only faces, complete back views. No new powers, weapons, encounter placement or named lore.
