# Water Elemental and Red Dragon

Final two older combat appearances, following Air/Earth/Fire. Scenario generated one concept sheet per creature; exact briefs, job IDs, asset IDs and completion receipts are in generation-record.json. PixelLab is unavailable, so pixel figures are authored with OpenAI image generation and technically packed from whole figures.

Water Elemental / Sea: heavy dark teal water core, turquoise curled wave volumes, sparse pale aqua foam edges, narrow forward-facing eyes and a continuous water column/pool base. No legs. Distinct from Air's pale wind ribbons. Natural Wave Slam uses a connected palm. Tidal Crush heaves a joined wave forward; Undertow pulls its forearms inward. Concept lettering and loose droplets are excluded from production.

Red Dragon: mature brick-red six-limbed dragon, four grounded clawed legs plus two dorsal bat wings, ivory horns/belly scutes, amber eyes and a long connected tail. Folded wings during idle/walk keep the horned head readable. Natural Dragon Jaws unfolds both wings while the neck/muzzle strike forward. Breath braces with half-open wings; Frightful Presence raises its chest and opens a menacing mantle; Cataclysmic Wingbeat uses a broad connected downstroke. Concept bipedal-looking roar sketch is excluded: production retains four grounded legs in all moves.

Neutral scale targets: Water about 2.4 m crest-to-base; grounded Dragon about 3.3 m horn-to-floor and 6–7 m nose-to-tail beside a 1.75 m fighter. Size follows the creatures' roles and existing lore. All eight facings, no faces drawn onto backs. Existing gameplay abilities, effects, stats, encounters and colour variants remain intact.
