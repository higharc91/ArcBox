# Vampire Bat, Undead Bear and Crystal Slime concepts

[Shared concept sheet](three-variants.png) · [Exact brief](brief.txt) · [Scenario receipt](receipts.json)

Scenario Nano Banana 2.1 produced one three-column 2K sheet for 12 CU. These are existing library variants, not new stat blocks or lore. Complete existing Dusk Bat, Brown Bear and Green Slime runtime figures guide family anatomy and game scale. The bat remains an animal with two rooted burgundy wings and tucked hind feet; the bear retains four grounded paws, ash-grey fur and restrained bone/moss details; the slime has a connected gel base and rooted crystal ridge. Front faces turn away in rear views.

The concept's approximate physical labels are design guides. Final game scale is reviewed beside the party and established family sprites; the existing giant-bat family remains broader than an ordinary small bat. Pixel figures and natural actions are authored with OpenAI image generation, without PixelLab or procedural recolouring.
