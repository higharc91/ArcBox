# Troll and granite Golem references

Scenario generated two production concept sheets in the existing Default Project of Higharc91's Organization. Exact prompts, project/model/job IDs and parameters are in `generation-record.json`; final asset receipts and 24 CU total usage are in `results.json`.

The existing boss stat blocks and ability definitions guide these designs. The troll is a large giant with natural claws, boulder toss and frenzy roar, not a club-wielding ogre. Its rangy hunch, hooked nose, long forearms, olive-grey skin, short fixed scalp crest and practical rope-bound hipwrap make it distinguishable from the refreshed armored orc. Scale intent is roughly 3m fully upright, about 2.6m in its usual hunch; these are production scale targets, not new canonical lore text.

The granite Golem is a large construct with blunt fist attacks, seismic slam and stone aegis. Its carved masonry planes, squared head, solid articulated joints, grey mineral flecks and restrained moss separate a built guardian from the still-unrefreshed irregular Earth Elemental. No floating rock limbs, glowing core, lava, faction emblem or biological breathing is introduced. Its roughly 2.7m scale target is reviewed beside the 1.75m party reference.

Pixel poses and complete-source corrections are separate under `../../imagegen/stone-and-mire-2026-11-07/`. Existing gameplay values, equipment, lore, projectile art, FX and audio remain intact. Only two visual ability bindings separate the roar/aegis gestures from the throw/slam, with client presentation timing aligned to their authored impact frames.
