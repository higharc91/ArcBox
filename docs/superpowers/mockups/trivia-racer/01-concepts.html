Speed is distance

Answer right and you move. The faster you lock in, the further you go: first correct +3, next +2, the rest +1. Wrong answers stall you.

  • Simplest to learn and build; one tap per question.
  • Rewards reflexes over knowledge. Unfair to remote players on a laggy Discord stream.
  • Leader tends to run away; little drama late.

Bet your engine on what you know

You see only the category first and pick a throttle. Then the question appears. Right answers move you by your throttle; a wrong REDLINE spins you out (back 2, and you sit out the next question).

  • Knowledge plus nerve, not reflexes: fair for remote players.
  • Built-in drama on the TV ("3 drivers at REDLINE"). Trailing players can catch up by gambling.
  • Fits existing widgets: buttons then choice.
  • Two taps per question; slightly longer rounds.

Kart-style items

Correct answers earn an item card. After every few questions there's a pit stop where you fire items at other drivers. Trailing drivers roll better items.

  • Most social: grudges and table talk.
  • Extra phase every lap; more rules to explain and balance.
  • Can feel random; knowledge matters less.
