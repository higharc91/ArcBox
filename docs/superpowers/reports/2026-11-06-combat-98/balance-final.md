## Measurement and protocol

Striker bands use damage per target-turn: each member’s own-turn mean HP damage per damaged hostile, summed over turns and divided by battle rounds. Multiple hits on one victim accumulate; off-turn damage belongs to the source’s most recent own turn. Raw damage remains in every detailed class table. No overkill or temporary-HP damage is credited. Other band measures are unchanged.

Tuning uses seeds 1–6; final judgments use seeds 1–20. The benchmark sweep is five encounters (`crypt_hall`, `crystal_cave`, `forest_ruins`, `river_crossing`, `warlord_bridge`), every level 1–10, sizes 4–6 and six deterministic sampled parties per size. The final report also checks all nine story encounters at their own levels, companion-filled size-4 parties, and the scripted rogue’s default-kit cap. Performance checks run separately from simulation.

The first 5,400-battle tuning sweep found 47/120 band flags. Intended-level benchmarks averaged 9.0–11.0 rounds and 86.1–97.2% wins. Those numbers are tuning evidence, **not the final balance judgment**. A second candidate is being measured separately before installation.
