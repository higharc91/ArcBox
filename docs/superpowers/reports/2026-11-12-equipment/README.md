# Equipment progression (#126)

Characters equip owned weapons, armour and shields from the between-session portal. Server checks ownership, class/subclass proficiency and two-handed/shield compatibility; saved battles reject equipment changes. Starting equipment enters the bag once on the first successful equipment action. The persisted claim marker survives remakes, preventing a kit-resale loop. Equipped gear remains carried, and the final equipped copy cannot be sold or gifted. Extra copies can be traded.

Tobin's Forge sells the weapon/armour catalogue and five +1 pieces after `sq_forge_done`, preserving the Cold Forge quest interaction. Existing consumable prices remain unchanged. Base equipment uses conventional relative prices; +1 pieces add 500 gold to their base price. This adds a gear catalogue without claiming to resolve the separately owner-reviewed economy issue #147.

+1 weapons apply +1 attack and weapon damage; +1 armour increases base AC by one. Every post-Chapter-I story fight has an authored non-consumable reward. Staff, sword, bow, light-armour and medium-armour bundles vary by encounter. Equipment is a distinct item kind with no consumable ability: it cannot be consumed or used by the battle AI. Existing icon atlas cells are reused without changing image pixels.

Validation: 6 game tests cover equip validation, one-time/save/remake ownership, shield/two-handed restrictions, Hexblade sword proficiency, all story loot sources, shop availability, sale/gift reservation and saved-battle rejection. A combat regression verifies the +1 modifiers and that gear is neither offered nor accepted as a consumable.
