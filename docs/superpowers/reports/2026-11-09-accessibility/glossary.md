# Wardenflame interface glossary — #176

- **TV / host:** the shared display. Player-facing instructions say TV.
- **VIP:** the player who controls the session and chooses standalone battles.
- **Leader:** the player who steers the party and makes leader choices.
- **Party menu:** the visible name of the VIP session menu.
- **Don’t react:** the same reaction decision on phone, TV and battle log (ASCII apostrophe in interface strings). Internal action IDs remain stable.
- **End turn:** finish the current turn.
- **Carry on:** leave battle results.
- **Concentration:** spelled out on ability tags, which use at least 14 px type.
- **Stock:** Unlimited stock, Out of stock, or N in stock; bag quantities say N in your bag.

Passive prompts identify the dialogue, choice, roll or party map to follow. Failed-move notices identify the acting player’s next step. Class labels before the first turn and the established reaction wording were verified as already implemented.
