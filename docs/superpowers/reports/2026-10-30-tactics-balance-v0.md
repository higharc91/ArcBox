## Notes on this run (written after the sweep)

Sections 1–7 above are the simulator's output, unedited. The command in section 7 regenerates them byte for byte (checked by running it twice). These notes are the reading of them that Task 23 starts from.

- **Role bands can't flag yet.** Only fighter, cleric, rogue and wizard exist, one per band, so each band's mean is its only class. Within-band comparisons start once Tasks 17–20 add the other eight classes. Across bands the gap is large: from level 5 the wizard deals 20–28 damage a round (Fireball, 25.5% of its legal turns), while the fighter deals at most 3.8 and the rogue at most 2.0.
- **The encounter doesn't scale.** `river_crossing` places six level-1 goblins at every party level, and encounters declare no intended level, so every level is judged. That gives 88.6% wins at level 1 and 99–100% from level 3. Battles at levels 5–10 are short (3.9–6.0 rounds). Encounter levels and the full roster come in Task 21.
- **Battles at levels 1 and 3 run long, and strikers deal little.** This comes from the AI's play, not the content. Across all turns, party members Dash in 23.5%, Dodge in 19.6% and Help in 20.2%, but attack in only 35.9% of the turns an attack is legal. In a traced level-3 battle the rogue (`skirmisher`) spent its early turns dashing, hiding and dodging, and left sleeping goblins alone. Fifteen level-1 battles and three level-3 battles hit the 30-round cap.
- **Abilities flagged as rarely used.** Each traces to the planner or to this encounter, not to a wrong cost, range or target in the content:
  - **The planner never plans it:**
    - `rogue.cunning_dash`: a bonus-action Dash is never planned (Task 14's deferred finding).
    - `fighter.action_surge`: the extra Action it grants is never followed by a second action.
    - `wizard.arcane_recovery`: regaining Mana isn't valued.
    - `rogue.steady_aim`: not valued.
    - `wizard.misty_step` and `rogue.withdraw`: escapes, which are only taken when they beat the opportunity attacks.
  - **The AI holds the resource:** `rogue.trip` and `rogue.poison_strike` each cost 1 of a 2–4 Edge pool. The `skirmisher` profile's hoard weight outweighs the prone or poisoned rider. `rogue.obscure` (blinded) is used in 14.5% of its legal turns. `fighter.menacing_attack` and `fighter.pushing_attack` lose to Trip, Precision and Feinting.
  - **A better option exists:**
    - `wizard.ray_of_frost` and `wizard.acid_splash` lose to `wizard.fire_bolt`.
    - `cleric.sacred_flame` loses to `cleric.toll_the_dead`.
    - `cleric.cure_wounds` loses to `cleric.healing_word`, a bonus action.
    - `wizard.ice_storm` and `wizard.scorching_ray` lose to `wizard.fireball` and `wizard.magic_missile`. This matches the SRD.
    - `wizard.chill_touch`, `wizard.shocking_grasp`, `wizard.burning_hands` and `wizard.thunderwave` need the wizard in melee.
  - **Nothing to use it on in this encounter:**
    - `cleric.turn_undead` and `cleric.sear_undead`: there are no undead.
    - `cleric.greater_restoration`: no lasting conditions on the party.
    - `cleric.death_ward`, `cleric.resistance`, `cleric.banishment`, `cleric.divine_intervention`, `wizard.hold_monster` and `cleric.spirit_guardians`: few turns where they matter against six goblins.
- **Data fixes in this task: none.** No flagged ability is unused because of a content error, so nothing was changed. Retuning the AI weights (hoarding, Dodge and Help against attacking) and scaling the encounter wait for Task 23.
- **AI item use is 0 by design.** Party members play with items off, and the monsters carry none. The companion's one-item rule is covered by `CompanionTests`.
