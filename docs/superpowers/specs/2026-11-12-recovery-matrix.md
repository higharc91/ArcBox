# Recovery services

Issue #150. A room supplies reliable rest and limited physical care. A temple supplies immediate, targeted healing without consuming a rest. Camping supplies free rest with uncertain targeted care. This preserves existing tend/rally checks and existing rest eligibility, including rests after defeat; this change gives services different outcomes rather than changing battle rewards.

| Service | Rest injury | Body injury | Spirit injury | Base price | Rest required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Camp sleep | All | No automatic healing | No automatic healing | Free | Yes |
| Camp tend/rally | Sleep clears all | Tend: one on Medicine DC 12 | Rally: one on social DC 12 | Free | Yes |
| Inn room | All | First remaining body injury, one per guest | Remains | 10 gold per guest | Yes |
| Temple cure | One selected | One selected | One selected | 25 gold per injury | No |

An inn room also grants the existing Well fed benefit. Existing haggling rules apply to service prices (10 → 9 and 25 → 22). Camping can cure spirit injuries on a successful rally; the temple guarantees that cure immediately. The inn can no longer replace the temple for that injury type. Injury ordering in the character record chooses which body injury a room mends, preserving stable save behavior.
