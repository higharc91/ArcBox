Helping Ivo pays the same 1,300 XP as fighting him; releasing Halvard pays the same 1,700 XP as the gate duel. Optional `xp` on beats and choices is nonnegative, applied to all saved campaign members, and committed with the completed beat. The completion ledger prevents replay or restart awards. Normal character level-up rules apply; new members still enter at the party's established XP floor.

A roaming group more than one level below the party pays no XP. Current mob/chapter respawning remains, so this closes the cheap-XP loop without changing world population or combat.

Include story-only and full-optional arrival tables. Never assume replaying story battles or roaming groups supplies required XP.
